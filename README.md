

## **1️⃣ Credit Card Analysis 📊**  

### **Overview**  
This Credit Card Analysis Dashboard provides insights into credit card transactions, customer spending patterns, fraud detection, and revenue trends. The dashboard is built using **Power BI** for interactive visualizations.  

### **Features 🚀**  
✅ **Transaction Analysis** – Breakdown of credit card transactions by amount, category, and location.  
✅ **Customer Spending Patterns** – Identifies high-spending customers and transaction behavior.  
✅ **Fraud Detection** – Highlights suspicious transactions based on anomalies.  
✅ **Revenue & Profitability** – Tracks revenue generated from different credit card types.  

### **Tech Stack 🛠️**  
- **Power BI** – Data visualization & dashboarding  
- **SQL** – Data extraction and transformation  
- **Excel** – Data preprocessing  

### **Dataset 📂**  
- Credit card transaction records  
- Customer demographics & spending behavior  
- Fraud detection data  

### **Key Insights 🔍**  
📌 **Top Spending Categories:** Identifies popular spending trends.  
📌 **Fraud Detection:** Highlights potential fraudulent transactions.  
📌 **Monthly & Annual Trends:** Shows seasonal spending variations.  
📌 **Customer Segmentation:** Groups customers based on spending habits.

## **2️⃣ Customer Analysis Dashboard 📈**  

### **Overview**  
The **Customer Analysis Dashboard** helps businesses understand customer behavior, purchasing patterns, and retention trends. This dashboard is created using **Power BI** to provide actionable insights into customer engagement and loyalty.  

### **Features 🚀**  
✅ **Customer Demographics** – Breakdown of customers by age, gender, and location.  
✅ **Purchase Behavior** – Tracks buying frequency, spending habits, and top-selling products.  
✅ **Churn Analysis** – Identifies customers likely to stop purchasing.  
✅ **Revenue Contribution** – Analyzes high-value customers and retention strategies.  

### **Tech Stack 🛠️**  
- **Power BI** – Data visualization & dashboarding  
- **SQL** – Data querying & analysis  
- **Excel** – Data preprocessing & transformation  

### **Dataset 📂**  
- Customer transaction history  
- Purchase frequency & revenue data  
- Churn rate & retention insights
